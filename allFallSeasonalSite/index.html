Crafts

Skull String Art

This is a fun and easy craft to make for Halloween. It's a great way to add a spooky touch to your home decor, and you can customize it as much as you'd like! You can use different colors of string, paint an impressive background, or choose a unique frame that stands out. You're not just limited to a skull, though! You can make any shape you want, like a pumpkin, bat, or ghost.

    Materials:

  • Approximately 1/2 inch thick wooden board (the original instructions use a 2x3 foot size board, but you can use any size you like)
  • Nails
  • Hammer
  • String or yarn
  • Paint (dark colors work best against light yarn, and vice versa)
  • Skull template (or any shape you want)

You can find templates by searching for them online and printing them out, or using a projector to trace the image onto your board. Once you have your template, you can start hammering in the nails along the outline of the shape. After all the nails are in place, you can start wrapping the string around the nails to create your design. You can get creative with how you wrap the string to make different patterns and textures. Once you're happy with your design, you can hang it up and enjoy your spooky new decoration!

Salt Dough Garland

This is a perfect craft for kids and adults alike! You can make a garland of spooky shapes to hang up in your home, or even make ornaments to give as gifts. The best part is that you can customize the shapes and colors to fit your Halloween theme! Switch it up with different beads, buttons, or other embellishments to make it your own. Additionally, salt dough is extremely cheap and easy to make, so you can make as many as you want without breaking the bank!

    Materials:

  • Flour
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Vegetable oil

You can find templates by searching for them online and printing them out, and cut them from the dough. You can also use cookie cutters using Halloween cookie cutter packs, which you can find around Halloween in craft or dollar stores. Make sure to poke a hole in the top of each shape before baking, so you can string them together. String beads in between the shapes to add some extra flair to your garland.

Paper Cat Craft

Another cheap, simple craft idea for anyone to do! You can make a single cat or a whole family of cats in different sizes and colors to create a fun display! You can use different colors of paper, or add some personal touch with markers or crayons. Using this method of paper strips to create a 3D effect is a fun way to make your craft stand out. You can also use this technique to make other shapes and designs, such as pumpkins with orange and green paper.

    Materials:

  • Construction paper
  • Scissors/a craft knife/a utility knife
  • Glue
  • Markers or crayons

Accessorize your cat with a bow tie, a collar, or even a little hat! You can also make a spooky background for your cat to sit in front of. Just make a simple cardboard stand to hold a piece of cardboard vertically, and glue on paper or other materials to create a background. You can also make a little scene with other paper crafts or cutouts.

Treats

Chocolate Bat Bites

An easy, no-bake treat that's perfect for a Halloween party! These cute little bats are sure to be a hit with kids and adults alike. You can acquire most of the ingredients at your local dollar store to cut on costs. Candy eyes can be found at most craft stores, or you can make your own with white chocolate and milk chocolate, icing, or an edible marker.

    Ingredients:

  • Reese's Cups
  • Oreo cookies
  • White chocolate
  • Edible candy eyes

You can even prepare the ingredients in advance for your party, so your guests can assemble their own bats! Just set out the ingredients and let everyone have fun making their own creations. You can get creative with the ingredients to make other characters and creatures, such as a black cat with an oreo tail, or a vampire with oreo hair and candy corn fangs. The possibilities are endless!

Frankenstein Brownies

These simple brownies are perfect for a Halloween party. They're easy to make and look devilishly delicious! You don't need to be a master baker to make these, and you can even get the kids involved in decorating them. Just grab a box of brownie mix from your local store, and follow the instructions on the box. Once your brownies are baked and cooled, you can start decorating them to look like Frankenstein's monster!

    Ingredients:

  • Brownie mix (and all ingredients required on the box)
  • M&Ms
  • White chocolate
  • White frosting
  • Green food coloring
  • Red decorating gel/icing
  • Bittersweet chocolate
  • Chocolate sprinkles

You're not just limited to making Frankenstein's monsters - you can create a whole cast of spooky characters with these brownies! You can make vampires, zombies, ghosts, or any other Halloween creature you can think of.

Ghost Pudding Cups

These spooky, single-portion treats are perfect for a Halloween party! They are easy to make and look hauntingly delicious. You can customize them with different colors and decorations to match your party's theme. You can prepare ingredients ahread of time to make another fun activity for your guests, and let them assemble their own ghostly graveyard!

    Ingredients:

  • Chocolate mousse
  • Milano cookies
  • Edible ink marker or black icing with fine piping tip
  • Decorative candy such as gummy worms, crumbled oreos, candy corn, candy pumpkins- take your pick!
  • Whipping cream
  • Candy eyes

This recipe is best made using a hand or stand mixer. Whipped cream can be made by hand, but it's much more tiring and difficult. You can use a box of chocolate mousse or make your own. You'll also need some serving cups or bowls to serve, which you can find at your local dollar store. You can also use small mason jars or any other small containers you have on hand.

Decorations

Giant Spiders

An intermediate level project that requires some basic crafting skills. This is a great way to add a spooky touch to your Halloween decorations for a reasonable price. Most required materials can be found at your local craft or hardware store.

    Materials:

  • Hot glue gun + sticks
  • Awl
  • Faux fur fabric
  • 16-guage stem wire
  • Foam tubes
  • Foam balls
  • Half-sphere wire ball (you can pick up some extra wire and make these yourself)

The size of the spider will depend on the materials you use, but you can make it as large or small as you like. This project is best done by/under supervision of an adult, as wires and the tools used can be sharp. You can include the kids in the project by having them help with the painting and decorating of the spider.

More Spiders and Spider Webs

Similar to the giant spiders, this project involves creating additional spiders and spider webs for your Halloween decorations. These spiders are smaller, and are perfect for filling in extra space in your decorations. They can be used to create a more immersive and spooky atmosphere, along with a fog machine and some eerie lighting.

    Materials:

  • Chenille stems/pipe cleaners
  • Toothpicks
  • Fishing line
  • Large needle
  • Small washers or beads
  • Monster mud (1 part latex paint, 4 parts sheetrock walling mud)
  • Bamboo skewers
  • Clamps or more foam to hold things in place while they dry
  • Red puff paint or googly eyes
  • Paint (for the body)
  • Brushes
  • Scissors

These spiders are a more manageable size for children to create and display. They can be made in any size or color, and are less complicated than the previous giant spider project. They can be scattered all around your decorations. Go ahead and create a whole spider colony!

Giant Tentacle Monster

Creating a giant tentacle monster is a more advanced project that requires some basic construction skills and tools. However, the outcome yeilds a truly impressive and terrifying decoration that will be the centerpiece of your Halloween display, and the talk of the neighborhood! The total cost of this project is much cheaper than buying an elaborate, pre-made decoration from Halloween warehouse stores.

    Materials:

  • Plywood
  • Foam board
  • Lumber
  • Steel studs
  • HVAC flex ducts
  • Foam pipe insulation
  • Latex caulk, liquid nails
  • Paint

This is a more advanced project, requiring more tools, materials, and skill to create. The process is much more dangerous, but you can still include the kids in the project by having them help with the painting and/or decorating of visible exterior of the tentacle monster. Mess around with spray paint and different textures to create a more realistic and terrifying look. Adding a fog machine and lighting to the base of the creature would put the cherry on top!

Cops and Robbers Group Costume

A classic but simple group costume that's perfect for a family or group of friends. This costume is easy to make, and most of the materials can be found at your local thrift store. The original article suggests ordering some of the clothing articles online, but you can also find similar items at a Halloween themed store, or DIY your own props. If you'd like or already have it, you can switch up the black and white stripes for an orange jumpsuit.

    Materials:

  • White/black striped shirt(s) (for the robbers)
  • Blue formal long-sleeve shirt(s) (for the cops)
  • Black pants (for both)
  • Burlap or cloth sack (for money bag)
  • Fake money (for the money bag)
  • Fabric markers or paint (for customization)
  • Prop items, such as handcuffs, a badge, or a ball and chain

You could make the money bag costume for a baby, children, adults, or even a dog! You could also make a "Wanted" poster for the robber(s), and have the cop(s) hold it up.

Extra notes:

  1. Do not try to replicate the uniforms of your local police force to avoid any possible impresonation charges.
  2. Avoid using official police force logos or emblems.
  3. If you use a toy gun, make sure the tip of the barrel is clearly marked as a toy (do not paint over the bright orange tip).
  4. Do not use a cap gun, as this could cause panic and confusion in public spaces.

Pasta Family Costumes

These costumes are perfect for a fun pasta-themed group costume! You can make a whole family of pasta shapes, or just a few for a smaller group. This costume is easy and no-sew. You can use hot glue and fabric paint to create the pasta shapes. You can also use different colors of fabric to create different pasta shapes, such as penne, fusilli, or farfalle. You can even make a "sauce" costume to go along with the pasta!

    Materials:

  • Foam padding
  • Yellow craft foam
  • Yellow spray paint
  • Scissors
  • Hot glue gun
  • Elastic band (for clothing)
  • Velcro

You can be as creative as you want with these pasta-themed costumes! Add sauce or cheese on top, or wear red clothing underneath to represent tomato sauce. You can also make a "spaghetti and meatballs" costume by adding brown foam balls to long strips of craft foam to create the spaghetti costume.
